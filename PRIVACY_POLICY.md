# Privacy Policy for Shubhakaryam

**Last Updated: August 29, 2026**

Shubhakaryam ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Shubhakarya mobile application (Package Name: `com.kundali.shubhakaryam`) and website.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Personal Information
- **Name and Contact Details**: Collected when you book services (name, phone number, email).
- **Address**: Collected for doorstep ceremonies to help priests reach your location.
- **User Account Information**: Email address and name for authentication purposes.

### 2. Usage Information
- **Booking History**: Service bookings, preferences, and interactions.
- **Device Information**: IP address, device type, operating system, and browser info.
- **Usage Data**: How you interact with our application and website.

### 3. Information We DO NOT Collect
- Credit card or bank details (processed via secure third-party gateways).
- Social security numbers or other highly sensitive government IDs.

## How We Use Your Information
- **Service Delivery**: To process and confirm your pooja bookings and connect you with verified Vedic priests.
- **Communication**: To send booking confirmations, reminders, and service updates via email and SMS.
- **Platform Improvement**: To analyze usage patterns and optimize our services for Hyderabad and beyond.
- **Account Management**: To maintain your profile and booking history securely.

## Data Storage and Security
All data is transmitted using SSL/TLS encryption and stored in secure cloud infrastructure. We adhere to the Indian IT Act and relevant data protection regulations.
