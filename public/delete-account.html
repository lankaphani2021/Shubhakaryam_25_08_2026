Shubhakaryam Data Deletion Request

You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data from our platform. Once processed, this action cannot be undone.

How to Request Deletion

Option 1: In the App

The fastest way to delete your account is within the Shubhakarya mobile app:

  1. Open the app and go to your Profile.
  2. Scroll down and tap on "Request Data Deletion".
  3. Confirm your request in the email that opens.

Option 2: Via Email

If you no longer have the app installed, you can send an email to our support team:

  • Email: lankaphani2021@gmail.com
  • Subject: Data Deletion Request - Shubhakaryam
  • Required Info: Please include your registered email address and phone number for verification.

What happens after your request?

  • Verification: We will verify your identity to protect your account.
  • Timeline: Your account and data will be permanently removed from our active servers within 30 days.
  • Data Removed: We will delete your name, phone number, email, address, and booking history.
  • Legal Retention: Some transaction data (like payment records) may be retained for a limited period as required by Indian tax and legal regulations.
