Delete Account & Data

Shubhakaryam App (Developer: Phani Lanka)

As per Google Play policies, Shubhakaryam provides a transparent way for users to request the permanent deletion of their account and associated data. Below are the steps you should take and details on how your data is handled.

Steps to Request Account Deletion

1. In-App Deletion (Easiest)

Open the Shubhakarya app on your device and follow these steps:

  1. Go to the Profile tab in the bottom navigation.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the screen.
  3. Tap on the button labeled "Request Data Deletion".
  4. A pre-filled email will open. Simply tap Send.

2. Web/Email Deletion

If you have already uninstalled the app, you can request deletion directly via email:

  • Send Email to: lankaphani2021@gmail.com
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request - Shubhakaryam
  • Information required: Your registered phone number and email address for identity verification.

Types of Data Deleted

Upon a successful deletion request, we permanently remove the following from our active systems:

  • Personal Identity: Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
  • Location Data: Saved home addresses used for priest visits.
  • App Interactions: Profile settings and preferences.
  • Service Records: All future scheduled pooja bookings.

Data Retention Policy

While we delete most data within 30 days of request, some information is kept for specific reasons:

  • Transaction History: We are legally required by Indian Tax Laws to retain records of financial payments for 7 years.
  • Past Service Logs: Records of completed ceremonies are archived for 2 years for legal and safety compliance before being purged.
  • Security: Anonymized logs of system interactions are kept for security audits but are not linked to your identity.
