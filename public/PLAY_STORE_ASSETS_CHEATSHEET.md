## 🔧 Quick Tools

### **Icon Creation (5 min):**
1. Canva → "App Icon" template
2. Use saffron/maroon gradient
3. Add diya icon + "SHUBHAKARYAM"
4. Download PNG 512×512

### **Feature Graphic (10 min):**
1. Canva → 1024×500 design
2. Gradient background
3. "Shubhakaryam" + tagline
4. Download PNG/JPG

### **Screenshots (20 min):**
1. Open app on phone
2. Take screenshots of key screens
3. Crop to 1080×1920
4. Remove status bars

## 🎯 Design Principles

- **Simplicity:** Clean, uncluttered designs
- **Consistency:** Same colors and style across all assets
- **Cultural:** Traditional Indian elements (diyas, patterns)
- **Professional:** High-quality, polished appearance
- **Clear:** Readable text and recognizable icons
