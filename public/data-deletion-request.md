# Data Deletion Request - Shubhakaryam

## How to Request Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data from Shubhakaryam. Here's how:

## Quick Methods

### Method 1: In-App Request (Fastest)
1. Go to your Profile page
2. Click "Request Data Deletion" button
3. Your email app will open with a pre-filled deletion request
4. Click send - we'll process your request within 30 days

### Method 2: Direct Email
Send your deletion request to:
- **Email:** lankaphani2021@gmail.com
- **Subject:** Data Deletion Request - Shubhakaryam
- **Include:** Your registered email address and phone number

### Method 3: Contact Form
Visit our website and use the contact information to request data deletion.

## What Data Gets Deleted

When you request data deletion, we remove:
- ✅ Personal information (name, email, phone, address)
- ✅ Booking history and preferences
- ✅ Profile information and settings
- ✅ Account credentials and session data
- ✅ Referral codes and associated data

## What We Retain

For legal and operational reasons, we retain:
- 📋 Booking records for 2 years (legal requirement)
- 💳 Payment records for 7 years (tax compliance)
- 📊 Anonymized analytics data (service improvement)
- 🔒 Security logs and audit trails (platform safety)

## Timeline

- **Processing Time:** Within 30 days of request
- **Confirmation:** You'll receive email confirmation
- **Deletion:** Complete removal of personal data
- **Retention:** Legal records kept for required periods
